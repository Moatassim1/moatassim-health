GG Gains is the calorie and macro tracker that turns consistency into a competition.

Every day you log your food, land near your targets and show up, you earn Consistency Points (CP). Your friends do the same. The weekly ladder shows who's putting in the work.

TRACK EVERYTHING THAT MATTERS
• Calories, protein, carbs and fat with clear target lines
• Scan barcodes to log packaged food in seconds
• Thousands of foods plus supermarket products, recipes, smoothies, soups and yoghurt bowls
• Vitamins and minerals, with warnings for supplements that go past safe limits
• Gym logging with 750+ exercises, progressive overload and PRs
• Daily weigh-ins
• Steps, walking distance and active calories from Apple Health

GET REWARDED FOR SHOWING UP
• Three daily quests that build real habits
• A weekly quest that earns a streak freeze
• Logging streaks, levels and rare titles
• Tiers from Bronze to Diamond based on your last 14 days

COMPETE WITH YOUR FRIENDS
• Add friends by username and race them on the weekly ladder
• Seasons that run for the whole year
• React to your friends' days and see who overtook you
• Fair play built in: under-eating never earns extra points

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Take a daily progress photo, saved only on your phone
• Turn your photos into a transformation video with music
• See where your weight is heading with projections from your real data
• Export your data to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and talk your progress through
• Training-day and rest-day macros, with suggested adjustments as you go

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Progress photos never leave your phone
• No adverts and no ad trackers
• Block or report anyone, and delete your account and all its data at any time

Consistency isn't measured in days or weeks. It's measured in years.
