Common questions

How do I add friends? Tap the 🔍 at the top of the app and search their username.

How do Consistency Points (CP) work? You earn CP every day for logging your food, landing near your calorie and protein targets, hitting your step goal, training, weighing in, taking your progress photo and completing quests. Your weekly CP decides the friends ladder.

Where are my progress photos? Only on your phone. They're never uploaded.

How do I connect Apple Health? In the iPhone app: Menu › Devices › Connect Apple Health.

How do I report or block someone? Open their profile (or tap ⋯ in search) and choose Report or Block.

How do I delete my account? Menu › Settings › Delete my account. Everything is removed from our server. See how deletion works.
